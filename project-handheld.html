Edge-AI device · Hardware + software

Handheld Edge-AI Crop Phenotyping Device

2025 – Present Kansas State University · FarmsLab Design lead

Overview

A portable edge-AI device that lets breeders and farmers walk a field and get real-time phenotypic insights on the plants in front of them. The hardware exists in two form factors: a handheld unit for walked rows and a ruggedized mountable variant for vehicles and carts. All inference runs on-device.

I lead the design and development of the device and work directly with the breeders and farmers who use it, incorporating their feedback into successive revisions of the hardware and software.

What I did

  • Designed the edge-AI system end-to-end: compute, sensing, enclosure, power, and software.
  • 3D-printed hardware prototype with a handheld form factor and a ruggedized mountable variant.
  • ROS2 middleware with packages written in Python and C++ for sensing, inference, and logging.
  • On-device 3D localization, detection, and tracking of plants at 30 fps.
  • Run a feedback loop with breeders and farmers to refine features, ergonomics, and workflow fit.
