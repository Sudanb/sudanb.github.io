FSR-Embedded Insole for Gait Phase Classification

2021 – 2023 Kathmandu University · Design Lab Author

Overview

An insole embedded with force-sensitive resistors to capture gait events, paired with on-device ML classifiers running on an ESP32. The system segments stance and swing and identifies specific gait phases from the pressure signal.

What I did

  • Designed and fabricated the insole: sensor layout, signal conditioning, and calibration.
  • Wrote ESP32 firmware for sampling, logging, and wireless streaming.
  • Trained and compared lightweight classifiers (logistic regression, Naïve Bayes, decision tree, random forest, SVM).
  • Presented at the University Scholar Conference and co-authored the peer-reviewed paper.
