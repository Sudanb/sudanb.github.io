3D Maize Instance Segmentation Benchmark

2025 – 2026 Kansas State University · FarmsLab First author

Overview

A multi-architecture benchmark for instance segmentation on a public 3D maize dataset, paired with a study of how well 2D image-based phenotyping can recover 3D ground-truth measurements. The work is written up as a journal manuscript that reports the benchmark alongside the honest limits of the 2D-to-3D regression approach.

What I did

  • Set up a consistent training and evaluation harness across several segmentation architectures.
  • Ran the benchmark and compiled per-model results into a reproducible comparison.
  • Extended the study to 2D-to-3D phenotyping regression and characterized where it holds up and where it does not.
  • Wrote the manuscript in IEEE format for journal submission.

Code

An end-to-end pipeline that takes segmented maize point clouds, projects 3D attachment keypoints onto single-view RGB images, and trains a regression model (EfficientNet-B3, ResNet-34, or a custom CNN) to predict 32 plant measurements — stem length, 15 internode lengths, and 16 leaf lengths — with multi-view evaluation across 75 camera views per plant.
