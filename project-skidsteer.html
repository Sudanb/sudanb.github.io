Skid-Steer Field Robot

2025 – Present Kansas State University · FarmsLab Lead designer

Overview

An under-canopy skid-steer platform designed from scratch for row-crop scouting and spraying tasks. The project covers mechanical design and fabrication, motor control and the CAN-bus electrical stack, and the ROS2 software running on the robot — bringup, sensing, and the custom controller that drives the four wheels.

What I did

  • Full mechanical design and fabrication of a ~125 kg under-canopy robotic platform.
  • FEA on key load-bearing components ahead of fabrication.
  • MATLAB simulation for torque and envelope estimates to drive actuator selection.
  • Four-motor skid-steer drive on a CAN bus; power distribution, bus protection, and pre-charge design.
  • ROS2 bringup and perception software for stereo cameras, GNSS, IMU, and LiDAR.
  • Modular C++ ROS2 controller stack (motor, estimator, and controller interfaces) with kinematic shaping and a minimum-turning-radius projection; runs on-robot at 100 Hz.
