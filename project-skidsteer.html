Skid-Steer Field Robot — Digital Twin & Sim-to-Real Study

2025 – Present Kansas State University · FarmsLab Lead designer

Overview

An under-canopy skid-steer platform designed from scratch for row-crop scouting and spraying tasks. The project spans the full stack: a CAD-first mechanical design and build, a CAN-bus motor-control stack, a ROS2 bringup and perception pipeline, and a digital twin used to study how controller behavior transfers from simulation to the real robot.

What I did

  • Full mechanical design and fabrication of a ~125 kg under-canopy robotic platform.
  • FEA on key load-bearing components ahead of fabrication.
  • MATLAB simulation for torque and envelope estimates to drive actuator selection.
  • ROS2 bringup and perception software for stereo cameras, GNSS, IMU, and LiDAR.
  • Modular C++ ROS2 controller stack (motor, estimator, and controller interfaces) with kinematic shaping and a minimum-turning-radius projection.
  • Isaac Sim digital twin with rosbag-driven replay for controller validation and sim-vs-real comparison.
