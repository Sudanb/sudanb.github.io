Camera Mount Validation in Simulation

2026 Kansas State University · FarmsLab Author

Overview

Before building a camera mount for the field platform, I swept a large set of candidate camera poses in a physics simulator against photorealistic crop assets and measured how much of each plant was visible in each pose. The sweep covered elevation, tilt angle, along-row position, and row spacing — tens of thousands of poses in total. The result picked the mount geometry up front, rather than iterating on hardware in the field.

What I did

  • Built the scene from real plant assets and placed cameras programmatically across the sweep.
  • Computed per-plant visibility ratios (how much of the plant stays in the frame) for every pose.
  • Analyzed results to identify the best-performing region of the (elevation, angle) space across row spacings.
  • Used the output to inform the mount geometry on the physical robot.
