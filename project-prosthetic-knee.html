Pediatric Prosthetic Knee via Hybrid Manufacturing

2023 – 2024 Kathmandu University · Design Lab Research Assistant

Overview

A low-cost pediatric prosthetic knee developed using hybrid manufacturing to reduce unit cost without compromising performance under standard loading. The work covered design, simulation, prototype builds, and laboratory testing to standard load cases.

What I did

  • CAD design in SolidWorks and simulation of candidate geometries in ANSYS.
  • Evaluated manufacturing modes and materials for an affordable pediatric target.
  • Led static and fatigue testing of prototypes.
  • Co-authored the resulting peer-reviewed journal paper.
