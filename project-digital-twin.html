Isaac Sim Digital Twin & Sim-to-Real Study

2025 – Present Kansas State University · FarmsLab First author

Overview

An Isaac Sim digital twin of the skid-steer platform used to study how controller behavior and actuation modes transfer from simulation to the real robot. The methodology drives a shared controller from a real rosbag inside the simulator and against the physical robot in parallel, then aligns the two streams for comparison.

The work is written up as an IEEE-format manuscript focused on the digital-twin build and the sim-to-real procedure.

What I did

  • Built the Isaac Sim 5.0 digital twin from URDF/USD, including physics configuration tuned for stable wheel contact.
  • OmniGraph scriptnode logging synchronized sim + real CSV data keyed to bag /clock.
  • Sim-to-real alignment pipeline: offline rosbag export + cross-correlation + merge_asof time alignment for repeatable comparison.
  • Compared actuation modes and drew practical conclusions about which transfer reliably between sim and the physical platform.
  • Rosbag-driven replay through an OmniGraph controller so a single controller implementation drives both sides.
  • Wrote the IEEE-format manuscript (methods, modeling assumptions, validation procedure).
